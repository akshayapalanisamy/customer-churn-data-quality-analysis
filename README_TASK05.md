## Method and limitations
All figures use the supplied 15 rows. Churn rate = churned / total × 100; retention rate = retained / total × 100; monthly charges associated with churn = sum of MonthlyCharges where Churn = Yes. Currency is unspecified. The sample is very small; associations are not causal and the sensitivity illustration is not a forecast.
