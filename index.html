KPI definitions

Churn rate
Churned customers (Churn = Yes) ÷ total customers × 100.
Retention rate
Retained customers (Churn = No) ÷ total customers × 100. Churn + retention = 100%.
Revenue at risk
Sum of MonthlyCharges for customers with Churn = Yes. The dataset gives no currency, so none is shown.
Average tenure
Mean of TenureMonths across the customers currently selected.

Sample-size caution: the Task 02 README describes a 15-record sample. Patterns here describe this sample and cannot represent a whole customer population. Several groups contain 2–4 customers, and contract type and payment method overlap, so differences should not be read as causes.
